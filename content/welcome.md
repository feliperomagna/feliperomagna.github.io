## Welcome!


This space functions as a repository for [works]({{< relref "works/_index.md" >}}), [research]({{< relref "papers.md" >}}), and also for [writings]({{< relref "archive.md" >}}) that I enjoy sharing on various topics (films, books, ideas...). 

Some artistic works provide a connection to academic publications, as they were created in the context of my doctoral studies or from research I conduct throughout my professional life.

It is also an ad-free and non-monetized space. The site was designed with open-source tools such as [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) and is hosted via [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com).

Whenever possible, I provide content in both Portuguese and English; other sections (such as the blog) are published entirely in Portuguese.

If you prefer, you can also follow me on the [Fediverse](https://mastodon.social/@feliperomagna) (and still on Instagram and YouTube) through the links available in this page.
